Silaai

It is a clothing platform that deals in both retail of fabric(Product) and customized clothes(Service). also they provide a platform to designers to showcase their cataloge and skills . 

The idea is it treats all of them as customers .



The Flow



**- product** 

seller list this fabrics with various details / qualities , consumers scroll through them and buys from them required size of fabric of required quality and color.

the fabric is delivered by delivery methods.

Then the customer receives it and can return it in some 3 days of delivery.

the payment have can be either pre i.e. online or cod .



**- service**

seller lists his service on platform say like a embroidery or tailoring portfolio. the user discovers him through silaai and then say wants to alter her saree into a frock for her daughter so 1st step will be saree reaching service provider then he makes in a frock and sends it back then if she finding some problems with it she will send it back for fixes and the no. of these fixes will be decided b/w them beforehand only.

The other case can be she wants a custom frock and dont want hassle of sourcing fabric herself so she asks silaai's some fabric seller for the fabric ...



**- Designers**

say a companies wants to gift employs a dress like merchandise so it finds a designer from silaai and hires her based on her price and portfolio. then based on the users demand she makes designs and based on her t\&c she do's no. of designs and rework those designs and send gives her deliverable say a svg file and gets paid .



Silaai also uses a escrow mechanism . and tries to discourage COD since they are risky for clothing .

Bridal wear and other segments are also targeted .
